How learning is measured

Duplicate images stay in one deterministic fold. The system tunes its anatomy threshold on the other folds, then scores the unseen fold, repeating this until every image has been tested out of sample. "Improved" appears only when those validation results rise without worse defect recall or false alarms. A regression keeps the previous better policy instead of applying the weaker candidate.
